About AI

There is no AI inside the Hypewall app. Nothing on your widget is generated or picked by an algorithm. We used AI tools to help write the app's code, the code behind our videos, some wording on this page, and to look up lines in the source texts. AI never writes, translates, shortens or paraphrases a line: each one is copied word for word from the named source and checked against it before it counts as verified. If you find a line that is wrong or out of context, tell us and we'll take it down.
