Why this isn't LinkedIn's “Past 24 hours”

  • LinkedIn counts from when a role reaches LinkedIn. We count from when the company published it on its own board.
  • When a role is reposted, LinkedIn can show it as new. Our timestamp comes from the company's board, and the app will keep each role's repost history.
  • Every link goes to the company's own application page, not to Easy Apply.

Where we use AI

This page uses none: the board above is the companies' own public listings, sorted into fields and levels by simple keyword rules on the job title. In the app (coming soon), an AI model reads each listing's text to pull out the level, the years of experience it asks for and whether it's remote. It can get these wrong, so the original listing is always one click away. Later, the CV check (coming soon) will use AI to compare your CV with a listing. It never adds a skill, job, title or date that isn't already on your CV. Earlyapply never sends an application, a message or your CV to a company. You apply yourself, on the company's own page. We'll name the AI provider on the privacy page before the app opens, and your CV only goes to it when you use the CV check.
